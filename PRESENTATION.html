INDUSTRY CHALLENGES · PAIN POINTS

Operational Hurdles in Conventional Highway Tolling

Why traditional manual systems fail on high-volume expressway networks.

⏱️

Severe Toll Congestion

Manual cash collection takes 15-30 seconds per vehicle, causing mile-long queues during holidays and excessive fuel burn.

💸

Revenue Leakage & Fraud

Cash slippage, vehicle misclassification, and cloned plates cause up to 12% revenue loss annually.

🔌

Network Outages

Remote plazas suffer fiber cuts. Cloud-dependent systems freeze barriers, causing expressway gridlock.

📝

Manual Registration

Long paper forms for vehicle onboarding, human typo errors, and inability to verify VIN/chassis authenticity.

🔍

No Real-Time Visibility

Operators lack live corridor telemetry, making incident response and traffic management reactive.

📊

Financial Reporting Gaps

No standardized government ministry reporting, manual Excel reconciliation, and no audit trail for revenue transfers.
