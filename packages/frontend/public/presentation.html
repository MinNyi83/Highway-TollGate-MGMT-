SECURITY & COMPLIANCE · FRAUD DETECTION

Dual RFID + ANPR Optical Cross-Matching

How our two-stage optical and radio verification ensures 100% audit integrity at every toll plaza lane.

1️⃣

Tag Interrogation

UHF RFID antenna reads the vehicle's EPC Gen2 windshield tag at up to 10 meters distance as it approaches the lane at 30–60 km/h.

2️⃣

AI Optical ANPR

High-resolution Starlight camera captures plate image, performs deep learning character segmentation, and recognizes Myanmar script & alphanumeric plate codes.

3️⃣

Cross-Match Check

Edge engine matches Tag ID with Plate number. If valid, toll is deducted and barrier raises in <100ms. If mismatched, a Violation Alert is logged with camera snapshots.
